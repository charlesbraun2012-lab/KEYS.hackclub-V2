Guidlines

R1Eligibility & originality
  • You're a teenager, 18 and under.
  • The work is original and made by you.
R2Daily utility & keychain form factor
  • Solves a real problem
  • Has a >4 mm keyring hole and is below 60x60x20mm
  • No purely decorative objects (nameplates or plain logos)
R3Time tracking & grant rate
  • Qualified hours earn ~$8/hour through HCB, for parts and manufacturing.
  • Lapse is required for CAD, PCB layout, assembly, and soldering .
  • Hackatime is used for firmware and code.
R4What counts vs. what doesn't
Counts
  • CAD and PCB work
  • Coding firmware
  • Assembly
Doesn't count
  • 3D printing / slicing time
  • Idle time
  • Work unrelated to the project
  • Making the Repo
R5Shipping & submission

Submit a public GitHub repo with:

  • Source CAD and PCB files
  • Step for CAD, Gerber for PCB
  • Code (if applicale)
  • A BOM
  • Lapse and Hackatime in the journal.md
  • A README with photos of the design

FAQ

How much does it cost?

Nothing. You ship the design, we ship funding.

I've never done CAD. Can I join?

Yes. Keychains are a great first project. Ask in the Slack channel and someone will help you get started.

What software can I use?

Anything that exports a Step/Gerber file: Onshape, Fusion, KiCad, Tracewright

Can I submit more than once?

Ship as much as you want, spamming low quality projects will lead to you being unable to ship
